* Set the working directory to this example folder before running.
GET DATA /TYPE=TXT /FILE='veri.csv' /ENCODING='UTF8'
 /ARRANGEMENT=DELIMITED /DELCASE=LINE /DELIMITERS="," /FIRSTCASE=2
 /VARIABLES=
 grup F8.2
 puan F8.2.
EXECUTE.
EXAMINE VARIABLES=puan BY grup /PLOT=BOXPLOT /STATISTICS=DESCRIPTIVES.
NPAR TESTS /M-W=puan BY grup(1 2) /MISSING ANALYSIS.
* Compare Asymp. Sig. (2-tailed); U in SPSS is the smaller of U1 and U2.
